02 — Where it grows

Mist first,
altitude second,
coffee always.

Every ARANYA lot is a single mountainside, not a blend of convenience. In Gulmi and Arghakhanchi, cherry ripens slowly on volcanic-leaning red loam — nearly twelve months from flower to pick. That slowness is the flavour: more sugar, more acid, more character.

We buy the entire harvest from 412 smallholder families, pay 38% above the Fairtrade floor, and cup every lot blind before we commit. What doesn’t make the cut becomes our staff espresso — brutal honesty, daily.

  • 412smallholder farms, paid 38% above Fairtrade floor
  • 11⅓months from flower to cherry — one of the slowest cycles on earth
  • 100%lot-traced: farm, altitude, process and cup score on every bag
