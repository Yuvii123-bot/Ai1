Before we begin

Every light-year of this galaxy is simulated here. Four thousand civilisations arise at random points in space and time, over five billion years.

  • They expand, meet, and make a choice.
  • Some choose to hide. Some choose to broadcast.
  • Some choose to strike first.

The "dark forest" hypothesis says that any civilisation advanced enough to be seen is a threat, so the only rational move is silence. Watch what the mathematics of that idea actually produces.
